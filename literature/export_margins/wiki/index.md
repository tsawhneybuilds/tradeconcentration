# Export Margins Literature Wiki - Index

This index covers **10 papers**. It is the token-light entry point for the export-margins literature workspace. The full converted text for each paper is in `papers/`, and the project-facing synthesis is in `../project_study_guide.md`.

**How to use this wiki:**
- Start here for metadata, reading order, and short contribution notes.
- Use `rg` across `papers/*.md` for concepts such as extensive margin, intensive margin, new goods, destination, survival, quality, and superstars.
- Open individual `papers/*.md` files only when a paper matters for a specific claim, formula, or empirical design choice.

## Recommended Reading Order

1. [[01_hummels_klenow_2005_variety_quality_exports]] - baseline product-variety, quality, and quantity decomposition.
2. [[02_kehoe_ruhl_2013_new_goods_margin]] - new-goods margin and least-traded goods.
3. [[03_evenett_venables_2002_market_entry_bilateral_flows]] - new destinations for established exportables.
4. [[05_brenton_newfarmer_2007_discovery_channel_export_cycles]] - discovery versus post-discovery scaling and geographic expansion.
5. [[04_besedes_prusa_2011_extensive_intensive_margins_export_growth]] - survival, exit, and duration of export relationships.
6. [[06_cadot_carrere_strauss_kahn_2011_export_diversification_hump]] - diversification over development, mostly through the extensive margin.
7. [[07_eaton_kortum_kramarz_2011_anatomy_international_trade]] - firm-destination export participation.
8. [[08_bernard_redding_schott_2011_multiproduct_firms_trade_liberalization]] - multi-product firms and within-firm product selection.
9. [[09_freund_pierola_2012_2015_export_superstars]] - superstar exporters and firm concentration.
10. [[10_fieler_eaton_2019_2025_margins_of_trade]] - general-equilibrium margins, quality, unit values, and welfare.

## 1. The Variety and Quality of a Nation's Exports

**Authors:** David Hummels; Peter J. Klenow  
**Year:** 2005  
**Source PDF:** `01_hummels_klenow_2005_variety_quality_exports.pdf`  
**Full text:** [[01_hummels_klenow_2005_variety_quality_exports]]  

Baseline decomposition paper for asking whether larger or richer countries export more because they sell more product categories, sell more per category, or sell higher-quality goods. Use it to discipline this repo's product and product-destination margin accounting.

---

## 2. How Important Is the New Goods Margin in International Trade?

**Authors:** Timothy J. Kehoe; Kim J. Ruhl  
**Year:** 2013  
**Source PDF:** `02_kehoe_ruhl_2013_new_goods_margin.pdf`  
**Full text:** [[02_kehoe_ruhl_2013_new_goods_margin]]  

Defines the new-goods margin using least-traded goods, avoiding a brittle zero-trade-only definition. It is the closest match for Exercise 12's `new_item` accounting at the product and product-partner-cell levels.

---

## 3. Export Growth in Developing Countries: Market Entry and Bilateral Trade Flows

**Authors:** Simon J. Evenett; Anthony J. Venables  
**Year:** 2002  
**JEL:** F1  
**Keywords:** bilateral trade, market entry, developing countries, gravity equation  
**Full text:** [[03_evenett_venables_2002_market_entry_bilateral_flows]]  

> We document the disappearance of numerous zeros in bilateral trade matrices since 1970.

The core contribution is a decomposition in which developing-country export growth can come from selling long-standing exportables to new partners. This separates product diversification from destination diversification.

---

## 4. Watching More Than the Discovery Channel: Export Cycles and Diversification in Development

**Authors:** Paul Brenton; Richard Newfarmer  
**Year:** 2007  
**Source PDF:** `05_brenton_newfarmer_2007_discovery_channel_export_cycles.pdf`  
**Full text:** [[05_brenton_newfarmer_2007_discovery_channel_export_cycles]]  

Separates discovery of new products, post-discovery growth, existing markets, and new geographic markets. It is useful for interpreting whether Exercise 12's growth is true product discovery or expansion of known products into more destinations.

---

## 5. The Role of Extensive and Intensive Margins and Export Growth

**Authors:** Tibor Besedes; Thomas J. Prusa  
**Year:** 2011  
**Source PDF:** `04_besedes_prusa_2011_extensive_intensive_margins_export_growth.pdf`  
**Full text:** [[04_besedes_prusa_2011_extensive_intensive_margins_export_growth]]  

> We investigate and compare countries' export growth based on their performance at the extensive and intensive export margins.

The key lesson is that entry counts are not enough: many export relationships are short-lived, so survival and scaling should be checked alongside product-partner entry.

---

## 6. Export Diversification: What's Behind the Hump?

**Authors:** Olivier Cadot; Celine Carrere; Vanessa Strauss-Kahn  
**Year:** 2011  
**Keywords:** export diversification, international trade, HS6, extensive margin  
**Full text:** [[06_cadot_carrere_strauss_kahn_2011_export_diversification_hump]]  

Uses HS6 data for 156 countries and shows a hump-shaped relationship between development and export diversification. This is the country-development complement to the repo's concentration measures.

---

## 7. An Anatomy of International Trade: Evidence from French Firms

**Authors:** Jonathan Eaton; Samuel Kortum; Francis Kramarz  
**Year:** 2011  
**Source PDF:** `07_eaton_kortum_kramarz_2011_anatomy_international_trade.pdf`  
**Full text:** [[07_eaton_kortum_kramarz_2011_anatomy_international_trade]]  

Firm-destination anatomy paper. It links market size, destination difficulty, and the number of firms serving each destination, which is the firm-level mechanism behind product-partner cells.

---

## 8. Multi-Product Firms and Trade Liberalization

**Authors:** Andrew B. Bernard; Stephen J. Redding; Peter K. Schott  
**Year:** 2011  
**Source PDF:** `08_bernard_redding_schott_2011_multiproduct_firms_trade_liberalization.pdf`  
**Full text:** [[08_bernard_redding_schott_2011_multiproduct_firms_trade_liberalization]]  

Explains why product margins observed in customs data may reflect product-scope choices inside existing firms, not only entry by new firms or new industries.

---

## 9. Export Superstars

**Authors:** Caroline Freund; Martha Denisse Pierola  
**Year:** 2012  
**Source PDF:** `09_freund_pierola_2012_2015_export_superstars.pdf`  
**Full text:** [[09_freund_pierola_2012_2015_export_superstars]]  

Shows that the top one percent of exporters shape sector export patterns and much of export growth. Use it as the firm-concentration caution when interpreting aggregate diversification or concentration measures.

---

## 10. The Margins of Trade

**Authors:** Ana Cecilia Fieler; Jonathan Eaton  
**Year:** 2025  
**Source PDF:** `10_fieler_eaton_2019_2025_margins_of_trade.pdf`  
**Full text:** [[10_fieler_eaton_2019_2025_margins_of_trade]]  

Adds product extensive margins, quality, quantity, unit values, and welfare in a general-equilibrium framework. Read after the accounting papers, because it turns the descriptive margin objects into model-implied welfare channels.
