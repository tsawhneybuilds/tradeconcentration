# Referee 2 Report: Historical Partner Gini Extension Design

Date: 2026-06-02

## Summary

I audited `results/historical_partner_gini_design.md` against the repository rules in `AGENTS.md`. No files were edited during the independent review. Verdict: **major revision before implementation or website publication**. The design correctly identifies coverage drift as the central threat, but the robustness plan mostly documents sensitivity; it does not yet bound or isolate the mechanical artifact well enough to support a historical trend claim.

## Major Concerns

1. **Coverage drift is acknowledged but not identified.** Add a deliberate censoring/placebo exercise: take high-coverage later years, degrade them to early-year partner-count or coverage patterns, and measure the artificial trend generated by source incompleteness.

2. **Gini over observed positive partners is not comparable when `n` changes.** Raw Gini has a changing finite-sample upper bound. The design needs a rule that Gini is undefined or non-publishable below a minimum `n`, plus normalized Gini or parallel HHI/effective-partner reporting.

3. **Coverage-ratio thresholds are insufficient and may be circular.** The ladder only works if `source_total_trade_value` is independently validated. Federico-Tena validation is mentioned but not operationalized as a pass/fail denominator audit.

4. **Common-partner-set checks do not solve missingness.** Baseline, endpoint, and either-year partner sets conflate true zero trade, unreported dyads, partner-code changes, and boundary splits. The design needs dyad-level observability flags and a decomposition of Gini changes into common-partner intensive margin, partner entry/exit, and boundary reclassification.

5. **Residualizing on coverage is not a robustness proof.** Partner count is partly a substantive outcome and partly a measurement process; conditioning on it can remove real diversification or introduce distortion. This should be labeled diagnostic only.

6. **Source-stack comparability is under-specified.** CEPII, COW, DOTS, Federico-Tena, UNSD, and national sources differ in polity universe, colonies, valuation, reporter/mirror conventions, and flow definitions. The source overlap audit must compare partner-level distributions, top partners, totals, and entity mappings.

7. **Sample definitions are not yet pre-analysis definitions.** `historical_core_1900` says "enough partner coverage" but does not define the threshold before implementation. The design must predeclare inclusion rules by year window, flow, minimum count, coverage ratio, and boundary continuity.

8. **Website risk remains high.** Any historical page must visibly distinguish design/provisional diagnostics from results and must not silently mix historical-core, COW-sovereign, DOTS, and modern `rd2_countries` samples.

## Minor Concerns

1. Top-K shares need denominators specified: share of valid-partner total, independent source total, or total including unallocated residual.

2. Rank-truncated Gini can hide exactly the lower-tail expansion being audited. Pair it with residual/unobserved mass.

3. Aggregate partners are excluded correctly, but the design needs a concrete source-specific exclusion-code registry before data processing.

4. War and boundary flags should include decolonization and customs-union/reporting-unit changes, not only wars and state succession.

5. The repo's `999999` convention is handled sensibly for partner-only concentration, but any product-coded historical side path must apply that rule before aggregation.

## Prioritized Revisions

1. Add a coverage-bias bounding module: treat missing residual trade as one "other" partner, split across many equal small partners, allocated proportionally, and allocated to the top partner; report the resulting Gini envelope.

2. Add a synthetic censoring/placebo diagnostic: degrade complete later-year partner vectors to early-year coverage/count regimes and quantify the mechanical trend.

3. Predeclare main-sample eligibility: minimum partner count, independent coverage ratio, allowable boundary breaks, required years, and whether Gini is undefined below the threshold.

4. Build a source concordance audit before source stacking: reporter/partner entity crosswalks, flow definitions, valuation conventions, mirror flags, totals reconciliation, and partner-level overlap checks.

5. Require every publishable graph or website output to show Gini, partner count, coverage ratio, source regime, and boundary flags, with clear labels that historical samples are not automatically `rd2_countries`.

6. Downgrade the residual fixed-effect model to a diagnostic appendix and avoid language implying it corrects measurement drift.
